Why Micro-Guides?

Travel independently, with local help when you want it.

Traditional tour groups are rigid and expensive. In Beppu, most travelers just need a friendly bilingual local to help order at a hole-in-the-wall izakaya, explain the hot spring rituals, or figure out the local Kamenoi bus schedule.
