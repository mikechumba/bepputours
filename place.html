Beppu Jigoku (Seven Hells) Tour

別府地獄めぐり · Geothermal Wonder

About this destination

The 'Hells' (Jigoku) are seven dramatic geothermal hot springs strictly meant for viewing rather than bathing...

💡
Local Insider Tip:

Buy the combined 7-hell ticket if you plan to visit 3 or more. Wear comfortable walking shoes. Kamenoi Bus #5 or #7 takes you directly from Beppu Station West Exit.
